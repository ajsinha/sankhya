# Runbook — `SNK-S0005`, invariant violated

**Class:** fatal. **This is a defect in SANKHYA, not a misconfiguration.**

## Symptom

`SNK-S0005` is reported. The detail names the invariant that does not hold.

## What is actually wrong

An internal consistency check failed — something the code proves cannot happen, happened.
Every other error in this catalogue describes a situation the system anticipated. This one
describes the system being wrong about itself.

**Failing fast is deliberate.** Continuing past a broken invariant turns a detectable fault
into a silent wrong answer, and a wrong answer from a system whose outputs feed
reconciliations is worse than an outage, because it propagates and is discovered much later
by somebody else.

## What to do

**Capture a diagnostic bundle immediately, before restarting anything.** The evidence is in
the current state. A restart clears it and the report becomes "it happened once and we do not
know why", which cannot be acted on.

Include:

- The full error detail, which names the invariant.
- The diagnostic run: `sankhya-server doctor`.
- The audit chain head and whether it still verifies.
- What was running at the time — a query, a compaction, an apply, a re-snapshot.

**Then report it.** This is not something an operator can fix from outside, and the detail is
what makes it findable.

**Restarting is reasonable once the evidence is captured.** Many invariant violations are
about in-memory state and do not recur after a restart. That does not mean they are resolved
— it means the state that violated the invariant is gone. The defect is still there.

## What to check before assuming it is transient

**Whether the audit chain still verifies.** If it does not, treat that as the primary
incident: the audit is the record of what happened, and an audit that no longer verifies
cannot establish what was done during the period in question.

**Whether the affected table reconciles against the source.** An invariant about data rather
than about control flow may have already produced a divergence.

## What "fixed" looks like

A version with the defect corrected. Not "it has not recurred" — an invariant violation that
stops recurring under different load is still there under the load that triggered it.
